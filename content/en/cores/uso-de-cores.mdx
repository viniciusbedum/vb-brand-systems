## The 60 · 30 · 10 proportion

- **60% neutrals** — white or black, in backgrounds and large areas.
- **30% text** — black, white and gray for content and structural elements.
- **10% petrol** — whatever needs attention: buttons, links, one highlight per piece.

Red is an exception, not decoration: it appears only for alerts and errors.

## Approved combinations

Contrast calculated with the WCAG 2.1 criterion. For regular text, the minimum is **4.5:1**.

| Text | Background | Contrast | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black (`#0c0c0c`) | White | 19.56:1 | ✅ Any text |
| White | Petrol (`#226789`) | 6.23:1 | ✅ Button text |
| White | Deep petrol (`#1b4c64`) | 9.27:1 | ✅ Any text |
| Petrol | White | 6.23:1 | ✅ Links and buttons on the light theme |
| Light petrol (`#3a8fba`) | Black | 5.42:1 | ✅ Links on the dark theme |
| Gray (`#a0a0a0`) | Black | 7.48:1 | ✅ Secondary text on the dark theme |
| Red (`#dc2626`) | White | 4.83:1 | ✅ Alerts and errors |
| `#2e80a8` | Black | 4.44:1 | ⚠️ Large text or icons only |
| Petrol | Black | 3.14:1 | ❌ Avoid for text |
| Light petrol | Deep petrol | 2.57:1 | ❌ Avoid |

<Callout type="warning" title="Petrol on the dark theme">
  On a black background, the base petrol does not reach enough contrast for text. Use light petrol for links and icons, and the base petrol only for fills with white text.
</Callout>
