## Structure of a post

1. **Solid background** — white or deep petrol, never both in the same piece.
2. **One message** — headline in Montserrat 800, uppercase, at most 8 words.
3. **One petrol highlight** — a word, a button or a graphic element.
4. **Signature** — the VB symbol in the bottom right corner, respecting the clear space.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Safe margin |
|---|---|---|
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 px | 80 px |
| Portrait post | 1080 × 1350 px | 80 px |
| Story / Reels | 1080 × 1920 px | 250 px at the top and bottom |
| Thumbnail | 1280 × 720 px | 60 px |

## Carousels

- The **first slide** makes a promise; the **last** brings the next step.
- Keep the headline in the same position on every slide to create rhythm.
- Alternate white and deep petrol between slides only when the subject changes.

<Callout type="tip" title="Graphic pattern">
  The grid pattern (in **Assets → Images**) can fill up to 30% of a piece as texture. Never place text directly on top of it.
</Callout>
