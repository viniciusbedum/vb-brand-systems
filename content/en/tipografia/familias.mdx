## Two families

The brand pairs **Montserrat** for headings with **Inter** for text. Montserrat is geometric and conveys the idea of structure with warmth; Inter was designed for screens and keeps running text light and readable. Both are free and distributed under the SIL Open Font License.

## Headings

Weight **800** (ExtraBold) for the logo and short calls; **600** for page and section headings. In uppercase, always with increased letter spacing.

## Text

Weight **400** for running text and **500/600** for emphasis and interface labels. Body at **14 px**, with 1.7 line height.
