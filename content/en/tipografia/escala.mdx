## Scale

A base of **14 px** for running text, with discreet headings and lots of space around: hierarchy comes from breathing room, not from size.

| Level | Size | Weight | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Page title | 26 px (32 px on desktop) | 600 | 1.15 | Page title |
| H2 | 20 px | 600 | 1.3 | Sections |
| H3 | 16 px | 600 | 1.4 | Subsections |
| Introduction | 15 px | 400 | 1.6 | Description under the title |
| Body | 14 px | 400 | 1.7 | Running text |
| Caption | 11–13 px | 500 | 1.4 | Labels, notes, metadata |

## Rules

- At most **three levels** of hierarchy per piece.
- Uppercase headings use letter spacing of **0.1 to 0.2 em**.
- Lines of running text with **60 to 75 characters** for comfortable reading.
- Never use weights below 400 on text smaller than 14 px.
