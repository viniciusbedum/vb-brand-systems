## Do not

<Callout type="danger" title="Inviolable rules">
  These mistakes break brand recognition. If a piece requires any of them, the solution is in the layout — not in the logo.
</Callout>

1. **Do not distort.** Never stretch or squash the logo; always resize keeping the proportion.
2. **Do not change the colors.** Use only the primary or the negative version. No red logo, gradient or outline.
3. **Do not place it on low-contrast backgrounds.** Light petrol on deep petrol, for example, has a contrast of only 2.57:1.
4. **Do not place it on busy photos** without a solid color area behind it.
5. **Do not add effects.** No shadow, glow, bevel or 3D.
6. **Do not rewrite the name** in another font, or rearrange the lines of the logo.
7. **Do not rotate the logo** or use it tilted.

## When in doubt

Use the negative version on deep petrol (`#1b4c64`). It is the safest combination, with a contrast of 9.27:1.
