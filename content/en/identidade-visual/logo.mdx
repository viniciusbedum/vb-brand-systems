## Clear space

Keep a free space around the logo equal to **half the height of the symbol**. No text, image or border should enter this area.

## Minimum size

| Version | Digital | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Full logo | 160 px wide | 35 mm |
| Symbol | 24 px | 8 mm |
