## What this brand system is

This is the living manual of **VB Brand Systems**. Everything that defines the brand — what it believes, how it speaks and how it looks — is here, in one place, always up to date.

It exists so that anyone who creates on behalf of the brand (you, a partner designer, a video editor, an AI) reaches the same result without having to ask.

## How to navigate

The content is organized from the most abstract to the most concrete:

1. **Brand Core** — purpose, values and positioning. The why behind everything.
2. **Verbal Universe** — personality, voice principles and writing examples.
3. **Visual Universe** — logo, colors and typography.
4. **Applications** — how it all comes together in real pieces.

Ready-to-use files (logos, icons, fonts) live in **Assets**, under the folder icon in the sidebar.

<Callout type="tip" title="When in doubt, go back to the core">
  When a situation is not covered by any rule, ask: "does this help someone organize their own brand more clearly?". If the answer is yes, you are on the right track.
</Callout>

## This is a demo project

This instance shows the brand system with a sample brand. To create your own, edit the `brand/` folder (name, colors, fonts, navigation) and write your pages in `content/`.
