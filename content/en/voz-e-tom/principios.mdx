## 1. Clarity first

Use the simplest word that says exactly what you mean. Short sentences, one idea per sentence.

- ✅ "Your colors live in one place."
- ❌ "We centralize the chromatic management of the visual ecosystem."

## 2. Show, don't declare

Instead of claiming something is easy, show the step by step. Concrete examples convince more than adjectives.

## 3. Talk to one person

Write to "you", in the singular. Avoid "users", "people", "the audience".

## 4. Always a next step

Every text ends by making clear what to do next — even if it is just "explore the colors section".

## Tone by context

The voice is always the same; the tone adjusts to the situation.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Social media | Light and direct | "Your brand doesn't fit in a Drive folder. It deserves a system." |
| Documentation | Didactic and precise | "Use petrol only on action elements, like buttons and links." |
| Error or problem | Calm and helpful | "We couldn't save right now. Your changes are safe — try again in a moment." |
| Celebration | Warm, without overdoing it | "Your brand system is live. Now just share the link." |
