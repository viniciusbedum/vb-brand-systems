## Post captions

**Before**
> 🚀🔥 INCREDIBLE NEWS!!! Revolutionize your brand with our disruptive solution!!!

**After**
> Your brand has rules. They just aren't written down yet. Today we show how to organize your colors in 10 minutes.

## Calls to action

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Click here | See the full palette |
| Learn more | Understand how it works |
| Buy now | Start my brand system |

## Product messages

| Situation | Text |
|---|---|
| Empty state | "No logos here yet. How about uploading the first one?" |
| Success | "Done! The new color now shows up across the whole system." |
| Login error | "Email or password don't match. Want to reset your password?" |

## Vocabulary

| We use | We avoid |
|---|---|
| system, guideline, asset | solution, ecosystem, synergy |
| organize, clarify, build | revolutionize, disrupt, leverage |
| you | the user, the customer |
