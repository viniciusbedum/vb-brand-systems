## Archetype: the Wise Builder

VB Brand Systems combines the **Sage** — who seeks to understand and explain — with the **Creator** — who turns ideas into structure. It does not impress with jargon; it impresses because, after talking to it, everything feels simpler.

## If the brand were a person

It would be that designer friend who sorts out your mess without judging. They ask questions before giving answers, show examples instead of theories and always end the conversation with a concrete next step.

## Personality traits

| We are | But we are not |
|---|---|
| Clear | Simplistic |
| Structured | Rigid |
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Approachable | Too casual |
| Curious | Scattered |

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick test">
  Read the text out loud. If it sounds like someone calmly explaining something to a person they respect, it is in the brand voice.
</Callout>
